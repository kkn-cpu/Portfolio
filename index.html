About me and my work

Petroleum engineering graduate turned Senior Amazon PPC Manager, with experience across US and EU agencies and now leading PPC in-house.

Email me

I'm Nikki, a Senior Amazon PPC Manager. I have managed advertising for more than 60 brands, from emerging sellers and medium-sized businesses to Tier A accounts, with single-brand budgets reaching $185K per month and portfolios of up to 15 brands at a time.

My career has grown from intern to junior specialist, senior manager and team lead. Today my work spans hands-on advertising strategy, leading a PPC team, and building the processes and tools that make PPC decisions faster, clearer and more consistent.

I turn inefficient ad spend into profitable, sustainable growth by fitting every strategy to the brand's goals, margins, stage and operations. Beyond ads, I optimize the listing itself: main images, A+ content, titles, bullets and backend keywords, briefing design teams whenever CTR or conversion needs a refresh.

Petroleum engineer by training. PPC strategist by trade.

I earned a BS in Petroleum Engineering at Palawan State University and went straight into Amazon advertising after graduating. The engineering training never left: I treat every ad account as a system to be measured, modeled and controlled.

Systems thinking

Budget, bids, placements and listings interact. I map how they connect before changing anything.

Test, then commit

Form a hypothesis, test it against the data, and only then scale it. That's how I run audits and bid changes.

Build the tool

When a process repeats, I engineer a tool for it. All 9 tools in the lab came from that habit.
