Min Clicks is the floor for acting on performance: if the primary window has fewer clicks than this, the row is held with no bid change. The zero/low-impression visibility raises are exempt — they exist for keywords that have no clicks yet. High Spend is the bleed ceiling: at or above it with no orders, the bid is never raised (held or cut, per the toggle above). Low Spend stays the low-activity marker — the visibility raise needs 7d spend under 2× this, and the gentle no-sales cut starts at 2× this.

Timeframe to optimize on

Original (cascade) picks, per keyword, the shortest window that clears its click bar (uses the min-clicks settings below). A single window (7d/14d/30d/60d) forces every decision onto that one window's data and ignores the click bars; keywords with no data in that window are left unchanged.

Data sufficiency — min clicks to trust a window

The tool uses the shortest window that clears its click bar as the "primary period" for each keyword. Higher = more cautious (waits for more data); lower = more responsive.

Amounts are read as percent or cents per the Optimize using toggle in Setup. ACOS thresholds are always percentages. Increase tiers are banded: each threshold is the top of its band (inclusive), and lower ACOS earns a bigger raise — e.g. with 35/25/20, an ACOS of 25–35% takes the 35% tier, 20–25% takes the 25% tier, and below 20% takes the 20% tier. Fields open pre-filled with the standard values — overwrite any of them, or use Reset to defaults in Setup to restore.
