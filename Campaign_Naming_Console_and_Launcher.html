Export bulk

Old vs new is a two-tab workbook you can read or send on — one tab for campaigns, one for ad groups, both showing the current name beside the new one. The upload file contains only the rows that change, written back into Amazon's own sheets with the original column headers and every other value untouched. Operation is set to Update. Campaign and ad group rename rows both preserve their IDs. Selecting a campaign also includes every changed ad group under it, and ad group update rows carry the final campaign name plus the final ad group name. IDs stay text.

Upload to Amazon Ads → Bulk operations → Upload. Renames take a few minutes to show. Check one campaign before trusting the batch, and keep the rename log — it is the only way back.
