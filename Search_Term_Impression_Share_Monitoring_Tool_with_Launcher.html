Campaign Launcher

Keywords in the queue

Paused keywords are relaunched in the new campaign, not reactivated. Keywords that are live by now are skipped. Leave a bid empty to use the suggested one.

Product targets

ASIN search terms from the loaded reports, best converting first. Tick the ones to target with the advertised product. The minimum orders and clicks above apply here too, except to ASINs added by hand.

Campaign settings

One campaign per match type and intent. Campaigns fill up to the number below and keep opening until everything is placed; a short tail joins the previous campaign.
Bids: the search term's own CPC over the performance window, then the CPC of the same keyword in the bulk file, then the flat bid; capped at the max bid.

Intent

Search terms with one of these words get the Branded or Competitor intent; the rest are Generic. The brand name from Settings counts as ours. Change any single term in the queue.
